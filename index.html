The problem

Passwords are essential to staying secure online — but a strong one is usually a wall of random characters and security jargon nobody actually enjoys remembering. A passphrase fixes both problems at once: a few ordinary words, tempered with a couple of substitutions, are far easier to recall than a random string — and because of their length, often just as hard, or harder, to crack.

Core mechanic

A password built from real randomness

Uses crypto.getRandomValues — not a seeded PRNG — to pick characters from whichever pools you turn on. Entropy and estimated crack time update live as you adjust length and character sets.
